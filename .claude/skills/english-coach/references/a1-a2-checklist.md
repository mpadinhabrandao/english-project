# A1–A2 sweep checklist

Purpose: the learner wants to "start from the beginning" without spending weeks on things already known. Instead of re-taking A1 lessons, the coach *tests* each item in 2–3 minutes and marks it. Items that fail become micro-lessons; items that pass are done.

Rules for the coach:
- 3 items per day maximum, inside the warm-up, so the sweep never eats the session.
- Test by *production*, not recognition: make the learner write or say two sentences using the item, or fix a broken sentence. Never "do you know the present simple?".
- Mark ✅ when produced correctly twice on different days. ❌ → add to `ERROR_BANK.md` with the item id (e.g. `A1-05`) and re-test a week later.
- Report progress as "A1: 14/22 ✅ · A2: 3/22 ✅". Expect ~4 weeks at 3 items/day.
- Skip A1 entirely if the placement test says B1 or higher.

## After the sweep

When 90% of A2 is ✅, the weekly focus moves to B1 skills (see `LEARNING_PLAN.md`, weeks 7–12): describing processes, connectors (*although / however / therefore*), second conditional, reported speech basics, the passive for processes ("the order is shipped when…"), and fluency work.
